Tab Keel Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Tab Keel does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything stays on your computer.

What Tab Keel stores, and where

When you save tabs, Tab Keel stores the web address (URL) and page title of each saved tab, the names you give your groups, whether you starred or locked a group, the name and colour of any Chrome tab group the tabs were in, and the dates they were saved. This is stored only on your computer:

  • inside your Chrome profile (Chrome's IndexedDB and extension storage), including automatic backups;
  • if you turn on the daily backup file (it is off until you do), in a file in your Downloads folder, under Tab Keel Backups;
  • in any copy you choose to download.

Tab Keel also stores your settings (for example, the list of sites you never want saved) in Chrome's extension storage on your computer.

What Tab Keel does not do

  • It has no servers and no accounts. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code.
  • It does not read the content of web pages, your browsing history, passwords, or form data.
  • Its pages are technically blocked from making network connections (a connect-src 'none' security policy).

Site icons in your list are shown from Chrome's own local icon cache, so displaying them doesn't contact any website.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Read your tabs (shown by Chrome as "Read your browsing history"): to read the address and title of the tabs you choose to save, and to close them after saving. Tab Keel only reads tabs when it counts or saves them for you.
  • Tab groups: to remember the name and colour of Chrome tab groups when you save, and to put the tabs back into a group with the same name and colour when you open them again.
  • Manage your downloads (optional, asked only when you turn on the daily backup file): to write that file to your Downloads folder and remove backup files older than the number you choose to keep. Tab Keel only touches files it created itself.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: to keep your saved tabs and backups on your computer without hitting a size limit.
  • Alarms: to run backups and remove items from Recently deleted after 30 days.
  • Favicon: to show site icons from Chrome's local cache.
  • Offscreen: to prepare the backup file in the background.
  • Context menus: to add Tab Keel's save options to the right-click menu of its toolbar button and of web pages.
  • Side panel: to show your saved tabs in Chrome's side panel when you choose to open it.

Deleting your data

Items you delete go to Recently deleted and are permanently removed after 30 days, or immediately if you choose "Delete forever" or "Empty now". Automatic backups inside Chrome are rotated and older ones are deleted over time. Uninstalling Tab Keel removes everything it stored inside Chrome. Backup files and copies in your Downloads folder are ordinary files that stay until you delete them.

Children

Tab Keel collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version offers optional paid features (such as backing up to your own Google Drive), those features will be off unless you turn them on, and this policy will be updated to describe exactly what they do before they are released.
